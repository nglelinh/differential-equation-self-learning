Chapter 12 Illustration Gallery

This page gathers the visual assets for Chapter 12 on functional analysis for PDEs. The figures are arranged to highlight the chapter's conceptual progression: measuring functions in Lp spaces, extending differentiation beyond classical smoothness, building Sobolev spaces, passing to weak formulations, proving existence by variational methods, upgrading regularity, and connecting operator theory, distributions, and geometry.
