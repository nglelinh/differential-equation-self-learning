Chapter 09 Illustration Gallery

This page gathers the visual assets for Chapter 09 on the heat equation. The figures are organized to emphasize the central narrative of diffusion theory: physical derivation, modal decomposition, the role of boundary conditions, reduction of nonhomogeneous data, Gaussian spreading on unbounded domains, Green-kernel intuition, maximum-principle geometry, numerical stability, and applications across science.
