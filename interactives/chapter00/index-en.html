Chapter 00 Illustration Gallery

This page gathers the currently published visual assets for Chapter 00 on mathematical foundations. The emphasis here is on the core language that supports the rest of the course: real analysis, continuity, multivariable calculus, linear algebra, and complex numbers. These illustrations are designed to make foundational ideas feel geometric and operational before they reappear inside ODEs, PDEs, and functional analysis.
