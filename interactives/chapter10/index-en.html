This page gathers the visual assets for Chapter 10 on the wave equation. The figures are arranged to highlight the central geometry of wave motion: physical derivation from force balance, finite-speed propagation, standing modes on bounded domains, multidimensional geometry, energy conservation, boundary interaction, dispersive and dissipative effects, and major applications in acoustics and electromagnetics.
