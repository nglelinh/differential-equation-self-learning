## Topics to Cover

- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Matrix exponentials
- Diagonalization
- Jordan normal form

## Key Concepts

For a matrix $$ A $$, eigenvalues $$ \lambda $$ satisfy:

$$ \det(A - \lambda I) = 0 $$

The matrix exponential is defined as:

$$ e^{At} = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(At)^k}{k!} $$
